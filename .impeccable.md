# Design Context: Morph

## Audience
One person and their phone. Secondary: hackathon judges watching a 3-minute
live demo on a projected simulator or handheld iPhone.

## Use cases
- Say what you need and get a real app on your grid within seconds.
- Watch it being built without reading a log.
- Change your mind mid-build, or change an app after the fact.
- Open, use, and delete apps like they are native.

## Personality
A quiet instrument. Confident, warm, unhurried. It should feel like a
considered piece of iOS, not a chat client and not a developer tool. The one
thing to remember: an icon appears on your phone because you asked for it.

## Constraints
- SwiftUI, iOS 18+, no packages, no custom font loading. System fonts only,
  which on iOS means SF Pro and New York (serif) are both available.
- Dark UI for the demo room. Warm-tinted neutrals, never pure black.
- One accent. Not cyan, not purple, no glow.
- Must read at a glance from across a room.
